RingPlayer Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

RingPlayer is a local audio player that remembers your playback progress. We designed it to keep your audio library and listening activity on your device.

Information We Collect

RingPlayer does not require an account and does not collect personal information from you. We do not operate our own analytics, advertising, or tracking service in the app.

Local Audio and Playback Data

Audio files, artwork, metadata, groups, favorites, and playback progress are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to provide app features such as library management, progress tracking, search, and playback.

Files and iCloud Drive

When you import audio, RingPlayer uses the system file picker. If you choose files from iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider may process your files according to its own privacy policy. RingPlayer itself does not upload your audio files to our servers.

App Purchase

Starting with version 1.0.4, RingPlayer uses a one-time paid download with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Earlier versions may offer the legacy Lifetime Unlock in-app purchase during the transition. App purchases and any legacy in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your local audio library, groups, favorites, or playback progress with third parties.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at lch0810@outlook.com.

隐私政策

圆环播放器（RingPlayer）是一款会记录播放进度的本地音频播放器。应用不需要账号，不收集个人信息，也不在应用中接入我们自己的分析、广告或追踪服务。

你的音频文件、封面、元数据、分组、收藏和播放进度会保存在设备本地，用于资料库管理、进度记录、搜索和播放等功能。圆环播放器不会把你的音频文件上传到我们的服务器。

如果你通过系统文件选择器从 iCloud Drive 或其他文件提供方导入音频，相应服务可能会按照其自身隐私政策处理文件。从 1.0.4 版本起，圆环播放器采用一次付费下载模式，不包含应用内购买或订阅。过渡期间，较早版本可能仍提供旧的终身解锁内购。App 购买及旧版内购均由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，我们不会接收或保存你的银行卡信息。
