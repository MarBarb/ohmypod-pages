Purchase model update — September 16, 2026

RingPlayer is moving to a one-time paid download. Version 1.0.4 will include all features and unlimited audio imports, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Existing App Store users will keep access without another purchase, and their local library and playback progress will remain on their device.

We plan to stop selling the legacy Lifetime Unlock in-app purchase on or after October 18, 2026. You do not need to buy that in-app purchase to use the upcoming version. If you already purchased it, your access remains available. When version 1.0.4 becomes available, update through the App Store.
